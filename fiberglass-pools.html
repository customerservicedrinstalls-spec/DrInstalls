How the Fiberglass Pool Process Works

DR Installs coordinates the full backyard transformation: planning, pool selection, excavation, shell placement, plumbing, decking, and final walkthrough.

01

Consultation & Backyard Review

We start with your goals, yard layout, access points, budget range, and how your family plans to use the pool.

  • Review available space, grades, drainage, and access
  • Discuss must-haves like tanning ledges, benches, steps, and deep-end use
  • Talk through timing, financing, and overall project scope
02

Pool Shape, Size & Finish Selection

We help match the pool model to your yard and lifestyle instead of forcing a one-size-fits-all design.

  • Choose rectangular, freeform, compact, or splash-deck layouts
  • Select color finish, steps, benches, ledges, and lounging zones
  • Plan surrounding patio, deck, fencing, and landscaping needs
03

Site Prep & Excavation

Once the plan is approved, the crew prepares the site so the shell can be placed accurately and supported properly.

  • Layout, excavation, base preparation, and elevation checks
  • Coordinate equipment access and material staging
  • Prepare plumbing routes, drainage considerations, and equipment location
04

Shell Placement, Plumbing & Backfill

The fiberglass shell is set in place, leveled, connected, and backfilled with care so the pool performs correctly long-term.

  • Set and level the fiberglass pool shell
  • Install plumbing, filtration connections, returns, skimmers, and equipment
  • Backfill and water-fill in stages to support the shell properly
05

Decking, Hardscaping & Backyard Finish

This is where the pool becomes a finished outdoor living space, not just a hole in the yard.

  • Add patio, coping, deck construction, or hardscaping around the pool
  • Blend in decorative rock, grading, landscape beds, and drainage improvements
  • Coordinate final details so the backyard feels complete
06

Startup, Walkthrough & Ownership Tips

Before we leave, we walk you through the pool system and the basic care routine so you know exactly what to expect.

  • System startup, equipment overview, and water-care basics
  • Final cleanup and quality walkthrough
  • Guidance on maintenance, seasonal care, and next-step upgrades
